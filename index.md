# Privacy Policy

**Randomly Championship 2.0**

**Last Updated: November 20, 2026**

## Introduction

Randomly Championship 2.0 respects your privacy.

The game does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or external servers.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Players can enter their names and save game progress, scores, championship statistics, and virtual trophies.

All this information is stored locally on the player's device and is used exclusively for the game's functionality.

## Internet Connection

Randomly Championship 2.0 works offline and does not require an internet connection.

## Advertising and Tracking

The game does not contain advertisements, analytics services, or third-party tracking.

## Data Sharing

The developer does not receive, sell, or share player information with third parties.

## Children's Privacy

Randomly Championship 2.0 does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the developer's support contact information provided on the App Store listing.
